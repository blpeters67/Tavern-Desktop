Tavern Desktop 0.1.6 makes update checks answer back. A check you start yourself — **Help → Check for Desktop Updates**, or clicking the title-bar chip after a failure — now reports in the title bar: **Checking…**, then **Up to date** or the download in progress. A check that fails no longer vanishes: the chip stays as **Retry update** with the reason in its tooltip, and one click tries again. Automatic checks stay quiet unless there is real news.

Includes everything from 0.1.5: the title bar stays clean until an update actually exists, a green arrow appears while it downloads, turns gold at **Restart & update**, and installs automatically when you close Tavern if you don't click sooner. Updates are checked on startup and every four hours.

Install this version manually once if you have 0.1.0 or 0.1.1; later versions arrive automatically.

The installer is unsigned.
